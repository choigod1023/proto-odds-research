# KBO starter exposure × replacement bullpen availability hypothesis

Pre-register one low-dimensional candidate, distinct from #658 starter rest and prior top-three bullpen workload residual. Hypothesis: expected early starter exit is more consequential when the replacement bullpen is fatigued or depleted. For team t, estimate pregame starter shortfall `S_t = max(0, 18 - shrink(mean outs over prior starts)) / 18` and bullpen shortage `B_t = sum_j(w_j * min(1, recent_pitches_j / 40))`, where j are roster-confirmed eligible relief pitchers, w_j is their share of relief outs from the preceding 30 days, and recent_pitches is from strictly prior two completed calendar days. The sole new interaction is `X = S_away*B_away - S_home*B_home`. Fit `logit(P_home)=logit(P_home_M0)+ beta*X`, one strongly regularized coefficient (L2 0.1); team draws handled under the same market settlement. No arbitrary claimed causal fatigue thresholds; 18 outs and 40 pitches are bounded scaling constants, not eligibility or treatment rules. Compare the unchanged paired M0 and market baselines. The hypothesis requires historic predecision verified announced starter and roster availability, completed previous games and pitcher IDs/pitch counts, model baseline, exact price, official results; teams with missing availability or incomplete source history are ineligible, not assigned zero. Split 2025 time-ordered fit and validation, freeze before any 2026 evaluation; do not select seasons, leagues, bands or thresholds after outcomes. Report paired date-bootstrap confidence intervals, coverage, Brier, LL, hit rate, mean odds, ROI, draw refunds and MDD, and all time splits and exclusion counts. If required data or sufficient predecision evidence does not exist, stop without fitting or fabricating outcomes.

Scientific motivation: Bradbury and Forman 2012, DOI 10.1519/JSC.0b013e31824e16fe, observed small workload/performance association in MLB starting pitchers; individual-velocity reliever fatigue model Lemasters et al. 2018 DOI 10.1515/jqas-2018-0007. Neither establishes KBO ROI or proves availability. Availability requires authoritative timestamped roster/health data, not inferred from last appearance alone. New metadata never modifies past predictions or frozen records.
