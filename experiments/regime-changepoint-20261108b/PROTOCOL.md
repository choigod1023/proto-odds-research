# Pre-evaluation protocol
2026-11-08. Four inputs are archive ledgers; include all valid/fallback rows, every sport and league. Binary winner conditional on non-draw; soccer draws excluded before updating or evaluation. Dates 2023-24 warmup, 2025 descriptive validation, 2026-01-01 through 2026-08-26 primary. No selection or tuning based on either period. League-specific states, fixed identical settings across sports. Processing ordered by day and ID, reveal day+4 before prediction, no smoothing. Models: direct market, market/form 50/50, fixed short/long residual control (event EWMA .1/.01 mixed equally), BOCPD beta-Bernoulli run distribution (hazard .02, prior Beta(5,5), maximum run 100, market-offset residual adaptation), and score-driven market-logit intercept (persistence .99, score step .05). All parameters fixed before evaluating. BOCPD observes home-win indicator, tracks deviation from market through run-wise summed expected probability; truncation discards and renormalizes the tail. Evaluate every league and all models, no retrospective filtering. Report league-level market slumps defined before evaluation as fewer than 10 market-favorite wins among the most recent 20 released results and league-level model underdog picks; these are descriptive, not additional independent samples. Select max probability, tie first, one hypothetical unit and Decimal settlement. Compare paired weekly clusters 1000 bootstrap resamples seed 20261108; difference candidate minus comparator; LL/Brier lower better, ROI/hit higher better; two-sided sign-tail bootstrap p is descriptive, not a sharp-null test. No deployment.
