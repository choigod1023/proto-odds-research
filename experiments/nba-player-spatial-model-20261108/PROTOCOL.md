For each category z let league rate per minute l_z=(league attempts_z / league player minutes) and league accuracy p_z=league makes_z / league attempts_z. Estimate the player's shot rate (a_iz+240*l_z)/(minutes_i+240). A player with fewer than five qualifying source games gets the league rate and league accuracy instead of a player-specific estimate; this is a fixed cold-start rule, and its projected-minute fraction is reported. Otherwise use player accuracy (makes_iz+k_z*p_z)/(a_iz+k_z), where k_z=200*league zone attempts / league family attempts. PN pools 2P and 3P zones first, each family accuracy prior totals 200; PS uses nine hard zones and the same 200 strength allocated within each family. If a zone has zero league attempts, its attempted and made contributions are zero; if an entire family or league minute exposure is zero, abstain. Clip probabilities to [1e-6,1-1e-6].
