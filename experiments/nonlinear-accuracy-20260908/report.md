## 방법과 해석 제한

- Retrospective exploratory archive; 2025/2026 was inspected in earlier experiments, not a pristine holdout.
- Archive odds observed_at and complete daily offer snapshots are missing; this is NOT a reconstruction of actual frozen T-30 site recommendations.
- Official result availability is approximated by next KST calendar day; incomplete archive schedules bias rest and recent form.
- No historical xG, starters, bullpen, injury, lineup or possession features are fabricated.
- Only supported full-time markets and their canonical favorite are assessed; partial periods and non-favorite reversal models are outside scope.
- Train separate sport/league/market/n_way binary favorite-hit models. These are candidate hit probabilities, not complete outcome probability vectors.
- Market logit is a fixed CatBoost baseline, with shallow residual trees, quarterly refits and a seven-day label embargo.
- Shrinkage is selected on pre-2024-12-25 out-of-fold predictions using a conservative one-standard-error rule; evaluation labels never select weights.
- Unchanged current UI recommendation functions are applied to reconstructed daily candidates. Missing pregame offer availability may change league ranks.
- Flat single-stake ROI is not combination ROI. Voids refund stake and do not enter accuracy denominators.
- Matched controls equalize pick counts within KST date, sport, league, market, n_way and 0.10 odds bins, not exact odds.
- Circular calendar block bootstrap and Holm adjustment do not remove researcher selection bias; production promotion remains disabled.
