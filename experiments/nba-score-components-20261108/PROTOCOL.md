# NBA score components protocol — fixed before candidate results

This is retrospective research, not independent blind validation or production advice. Use ESPN scoreboard/summary caches and receipts from the prior 83928e36 experiment, whose parser is copied verbatim under a distinct module name. Compare exactly its eligible 487 validation and 773 evaluation rows; both periods have already been examined. Require the same exact price vector and settlement; no filters or tuning. ESPN is not official NBA data. Prices lack verified receipt time, product identity and overtime rules, so ROI is conditional and historical only.

Each team’s last ten actual games through target date D-4 are selected before box validity is checked. Any missing/inconsistent box makes the target ineligible; no replacement with older games. Verify event identity, teams, scores and receipt hashes and fail closed on ID collisions. Target scores and boxes are never inputs. The prior experiment's identity, prices, labels and three comparators remain unchanged; independently verify the input join. Evaluation A: 2025-09-01–12-31, priors estimated solely from 2025-01-01–08-28. Evaluation B: 2026-01-01–10-27, priors estimated solely through 2025-12-28. Histories advance only through D-4. Aug 29–31 and Dec 29–31 are not training labels. These are retrospective refits, not a new blind test.

One generative candidate G, no market mixture. A team box consists of 2PA, 2PM, 3PA, 3PM, FTA, FTM, ORB, TOV, where 2PA=FGA-3PA and 2PM=FGM-3PM. Estimated opportunities P=FGA-ORB+TOV+0.44 FTA (not official possessions). For each side average own and opponent P over its 10 games; predicted shared opportunities are the geometric mean of both teams' estimates. For each side and each component rate, combine the team's offense and opponent's defense multiplicatively relative to the league training prior: r = prior * (offense/prior)*(defense/prior). Each numerator and denominator is aggregated over ten games, shrunk toward league prior with prior exposure of 500 opportunities for attempt rates and 200 attempts for shooting rates. Clip 2P/3P accuracy to [0.20,0.80], FT accuracy to [0.40,0.95], and attempt rates per opportunity to [0.01,1.5]. ORB and TOV contribute to P only; do not add extra possessions or points. Model 2PA, 3PA and FTA as independent Poisson counts with means P*r; scoring makes are binomial conditional on attempts, equivalently independent Poisson with means P*r*accuracy. Simulate each side's score as 2*Poisson(made2)+3*Poisson(made3)+Poisson(madeFT), independently. The distribution thus includes count and shooting variance but does not claim identification of physical transitions, player effects or official possessions. A tied simulated score is split equally between home and away, a symmetric overtime approximation independent of observed overtime; choose home at exact probability 0.5. Use analytic convolution of Poisson distributions (truncation 0..250 made counts, tail checked) to avoid Monte Carlo noise. No market offset or additional candidate.

Use identical valid odds and argmax rule as existing work, one unit per game, home on ties. Report per-period and month hit, ROI, log loss, Brier, mean selected odds, coverage and chronological daily maximum drawdown, differences and changed picks. Paired bootstrap resamples dates with replacement, 2,000 replicates, seed 20261108, percentile 95% intervals for differences versus market and both existing models. Do not infer actual wagers, profitability, or other markets from these data.
