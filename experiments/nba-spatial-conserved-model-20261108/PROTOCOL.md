# NBA conserved spatial structure — preregistered protocol

This retrospective experiment responds to an observed structural issue: the earlier S independently combines zone attempt rates and their sum need not equal the prior nonspatial Q family rate. Such volume drift can confound a comparison intended to isolate spatial allocation and shooting accuracy. It is a testable mechanism, not proof that this mechanism caused previous predictive loss. Provider geometry remains an unofficial coordinate proxy; historical input and prices do not establish actual pregame availability.

Freeze exactly four candidates before evaluation: hard conserved H, fuzzy conserved F, and market-residual anchors H_A and F_A. The previous 1,185 validated game source pool, old Q/S/residual, market and outcome_form predictions, their 1,260 evaluation events and odds are frozen. Do not filter primary targets on their own PBP or tune based on any results. The 378 whose own event belongs to the source pool form only a descriptive post-hoc subset. Existing 2025 A 487 and 2026 B 773 predictions and 721/1,187 training IDs are the common cohorts. Compare all candidates on identical rows; hard/fuzzy source selection must not change family counts, FTA, FTM, opportunities, source game/team identity or event history membership.

For target date D use all pool games whose KST dates are D-365 through D-4 inclusive; require at least 5 games per team, compute league priors over that same causal window. No target shooting, target score or future source event may enter features. Opportunity P = FGA - ORB + TOV + .44 FTA, not official possessions. Compute the same shared P and FT rate/accuracy as Q; no extra rebound possession. Shot counts and accuracy use the earlier protocol's prior exposures 500 and 200, per-family allocation of accuracy prior 200, epsilon 1e-6 probability clip, zero-prior category contribution zero, [0,1.5] clipping of each combined attempt rate, and fixed geometric shared opportunity. Q's family rate R_f is computed by pooling its family prior/offense/defense attempt counts before the old attempt-rate shrink/multiplicative matchup formula. For each family f in {2P,3P} independently compute old S zone matchup rates r_z and matchup accuracies a_z using its unaltered shrinkage and odds-scale combination. Set w_z=r_z/sum_{j in f} r_j and made rate M_f=R_f*sum_z(w_z*a_z); fail closed if family denominator is zero. Hence sum_z R_f*w_z=R_f, preventing attempt-volume inflation while preserving conditional spatial composition. Shared FT and Poisson score construction are unchanged; symmetric tied-score half-home win approximates overtime and home is the deterministic selection tie rule.

H uses the previous hard nine-zone attempts/makes. F uses the same hard zone boundaries but fuzzy 1-foot boundary membership: for a boundary b, distance r in [b-1,b+1] gives fraction (b+1-r)/2 to the lower-distance zone and (r-(b-1))/2 to the higher-distance zone; outside boundary bands use the hard zone. Boundaries are 4,10,16 ft for 2P and 24,27 ft for 3P, unknown stays unknown. Each shot distributes exactly total weight 1 within its point family, including made-shot weight; FT and P stay identical. These distances are an ESPN internal proxy, not certified NBA geometry. Floating input arithmetic must not change match/outcome eligibility.

H_A and F_A use logit(p)=logit(p_market)+beta*(logit(p_H/F)-logit(p_Q)), clipped 1e-6 to 1-1e-6, no intercept; beta minimizes sum log loss+10 beta²/2 by bounded scalar [-10,10], xatol 1e-12. Each training feature independently uses its own as-of D-4 history and league prior; A trains 2025-01-01–08-28, B refits 2025-01-01–12-28. Use the previous fixed training identities and assert full input matching, no beta grid, later feature, extra home bonus, odds cutoff, or candidate selection. These are previously examined periods, not new blind data.

Preserve per-game score-distribution probabilities, identity, original odds and original predictions, source IDs, features, prior metadata, trained beta, selections; deterministic gzip and input provenance hashes. Report all four candidates and frozen Q, old S, old residual, market and outcome_form, with period and month hit rate, ROI, log loss, Brier, mean odds, daily-close maximum drawdown, price coverage, changed selections, and paired-date bootstrap 95% intervals (2,000 replicates seed 20261108), including each candidate minus Q, old S and market. Explicitly report whether a candidate jointly improves hit and ROI in each period versus each comparator; no selective months. Subset membership is descriptive, not a pregame filter. Product identity, price receipt timing and overtime/settlement rules are unverified; one-unit ROI is conditional retrospective simulation, not evidence of profit, official product validity or operating-vote/M0 performance. No deployment or operational changes.
