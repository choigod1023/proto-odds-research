# KBL official capture protocol

- Population: official KBL match/list, KST 2025-10-01 through 2026-04-30, regular season `S47G01` only. Capture the entire fixture census before selection; include every matching scheduled fixture regardless of score or result. Record nonmatches and unknown statuses. Select 120 uniformly spaced indices `floor(i*(N-1)/119)` for i=0..119 in chronological `(gameDate, gameStart, gmkey)` order (all if N<120), without using scores or outcomes. Fix the selected ids before looking at boxscores. This is a census-derived sample, not all KBL fixtures.
- Fetch public official game header and player-stat for each selected id, serially, max 500 requests overall, timeout 15 seconds, at least 1 second between starts. Stop on 403 or 429 without retries. Checkpoint each response, lock against duplicate collector, and resume from cache. No credentials or bypass.
- Raw response bytes, URI/parameters, received_at UTC, status, SHA256. Git treats evidence as binary. Current receipt of historical records is retrospective only, never a pregame receipt. All match outcomes and individual game statistics reside in postgame evidence; recorded starter flag is retrospective, and DNP is not injury. Separate schedule census from postgame analysis when deriving features; prior-games-only history requires independently demonstrated receipt before the later decision.
- Match product, official game, date, home and away identity and all selections before joining existing raw odds. No assumption about buyability or timestamp, and no ROI or model fitting. Draw, win-by-1-to-5, handicap and totals remain different products; basketball middle-five outcome is not draw. Preserve overtime and per-product settlement terms.
