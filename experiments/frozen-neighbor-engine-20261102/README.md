# Input contract and handoff (research only)

No new-period results are included or calculated by this package. The caller must perform an independent source audit and fix the evaluation cohort before invoking `evaluate`. This package neither reads a network nor generates DC features. The fixed 2024 bank file must be the complete original #490 `oof.jsonl`, SHA256 after CRLF→LF normalization `158cd931f3fa576b97678dfcc0b44042b9ef1a5826183853fb6d9b0beb314f05`. `load_bank` checks the hash and parses only 2024 rows, yielding 228 immutable records. Source code versions: #490 head `a10cec7238b246dd8cc028b617268793e928511e`, residual neighbor #500 implementation `94d6702`; `src/devig.py` is unchanged from #490 SHA256 `2a102be60e3e07c03644afff1480f72e07d2fe1d08165f98cfed5bc5b29f31c9`.

`evaluate(opportunities, load_bank(bank_path))` accepts a list, one entry per audited opportunity; duplicate IDs or identity conflicts abort the whole batch, not silently drop. An excluded opportunity has exactly `id`, `eligibility: "excluded"`, and nonempty `reason`. An eligible opportunity has exactly the fields below:

- `id`: canonical unique match ID; `eligibility: "eligible"`; `day`: `YYYY-MM-DD` in 2026 or later; `kickoff`: ISO 8601 timezone-aware local timestamp matching `day`; `home_id`, `away_id`: distinct canonical team IDs; `identity_evidence`: nonempty audit reference.
- `competition: "KLEAGUE1"`; `market: "full_time_1x2"`; `selection_order: ["home", "draw", "away"]`; `prices`: three finite decimal strings greater than 1 from the same product; `price_source_sha256`: lowercase SHA-256 of original price file.
- `price_time_kind`: `"observed_at"` with timezone-aware `price_time` no later than kickoff minus 30 minutes, or `"record_date_only"` with ISO date no later than the match day. Date-only evidence does not prove pre-match receipt or price availability; audit independently. Never invent timestamps.
- `dc_p`: three positive probabilities summing to one; `dc_model: "kleague_dc_half180_l2_10_monthly_lag7_rho_fixed"`; `dc_reference`: first day of match month; `dc_training_cutoff`: reference minus seven days; `dc_training_sha256`: lowercase SHA-256 of source history. These declarations are not proof. Record actual original official finished-fixture source, identity mapping, fit code/model revision, versioned parameters/rho bounds, training cutoff, rows and state hash separately. The exact #490 reference is `src/research_kleague_dc.py` (SHA256 `e5678bf636a826dc830a9c981c8533c20456d9fab34bc5fb3de1f505c59d4e53` in #490); its fit requires at least 200 unique past official fixtures, rho bounds [-0.15, 0.025] and monthly inclusive cutoff. Complete dated fixtures and their availability must first be recovered and independently checked; absent those, **DC generation and real evaluation remain blocked**. No market-only fallback or target-label refit.
- `result_status: "official_final"`; `home_score`, `away_score`: nonnegative integers; `official_result_evidence`: reference to final official source. No other statuses are accepted as eligible; ambiguous/cancelled/void cases must appear as exclusions with reason. The evaluator derives the class from scores and passes only market and DC probability vectors to `forecast`.

All candidates share the same eligible set and bet once per event. Output separates opportunities, eligible, exclusions, and both eligible and total-opportunity coverage. Week bootstrap intervals need at least two calendar weeks. The unit ledger is hypothetical: it is not the real simulated wallet and does not implement or alter its monthly 50,000 KRW loss cap. Price source timestamps, official status, identity evidence, DC provenance and completeness must be audited externally; schema checks cannot prove them. Archive/source historical use may make a 2026 period non-blind.

Reproduce synthetic tests: `python -m pytest -q tests/test_frozen_neighbor_engine_20261102.py`. Reproduce source-only verification with `python -X utf8 experiments/frozen-neighbor-engine-20261102/verify_bank.py <original-oof.jsonl>`; it checks historical bank integrity, market calculation and the fixed formula on synthetic feature vectors only. Never supply new-period result files to this verifier.
