# NBA ESPN retrospective protocol — fixed before model results

Input: ESPN public NBA scoreboard and, when available, summary team boxes; domestic NBA win/loss prices. 2025-01–08 fit, 2025-09–12 validation, 2026-01–10-27 holdout. All source games must precede the target KST date by at least four calendar days, with at least 10 valid prior games per team; use only latest ten. Scores and box stats of the target never enter its features. History may include games without prices. Date and explicit team aliases are joined uniquely, and outcome conflicts and ambiguous names are excluded and counted, without outcome-driven mapping.

One candidate: home market no-vig logit plus a single home-minus-away net offensive efficiency feature, where team offensive efficiency is 100 * points / (FGA - offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.44*FTA); defensive efficiency is opponent points / opponent estimated possessions. Average the last ten game efficiencies; net = offense minus defense. Fit coefficient only (no intercept) by logistic likelihood with L2 penalty 10, standardizing using fit data only. No tuning based on outcomes. Refit on fit plus validation for holdout using its own training scale. If continuous complete ORB/TOV history cannot be acquired, use one shooting-efficiency feature: mean own eFG minus opponent eFG, eFG=(FGM+0.5*3PM)/FGA. This fallback decision is based on input availability, not results. Market-only is the comparator; choose higher probability side, home on ties; compute one-unit conditional historical ROI, hit rate, log loss, Brier, mean odds, coverage, drawdown, monthly performance and paired date bootstrap 2000, seed 20261108. No wagering filter or winner deletion.

ESPN is an unofficial publisher, retrieved retrospectively; four-day lag is a conservative historical proxy, not proof of pregame availability. Product identity, odds timestamp and settlement/overtime rules are unverified. Prior work saw these periods; this is not a blind prospective validation. The archive has irreversible text corruption; ambiguous aliases are not inferred from outcomes. No automated collection authorization or republication rights are implied by HTTP responses.
