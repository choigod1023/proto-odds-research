# Pre-registered KOVO source/model protocol

One prospective hypothesis: team serve-receive efficiency produces information not in the saved pregame market. For each team use only previously received official completed match records: exponentially weighted receive-efficiency residual (opponent adjusted by a ridge-regularized team/opponent model, half-life 10 matches). Separately for each league, fit `logit(P(home win)) = logit(P_market) + intercept + beta*(strength_home-strength_away)` on 2025 only; ridge penalty 1, minimum 30 complete prior matches per league, train through Sep 2025, validate Oct-Dec 2025, evaluate all 2026 matched full-match winner offers. Compare identical fixtures/odds/outcomes with market; 1 unit, no refitting to test, date-block paired CI 2000 seed 20261108; measure accuracy, ROI, LL, Brier, coverage, drawdown and average odds. Earlier match data must have actual response completion <= current prediction time and prior match finished; no same-day backfill from current response as historic availability. Require known unique league, official match ID, date, both teams, match final, receive metric/denominator, price and receipt, result/settlement rule. Source timestamps unavailable means prospective validation blocked; small post-hoc receipts only establish extraction, not historical availability. No candidate substitution or benefit claims. Hypothesis motivated by https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01083 and https://doi.org/10.1515/jqas-2013-0098; no claim those papers prove a KOVO market edge. Model is not PR656 20% set-win blend or set residual. No H/UO and no operations change.
