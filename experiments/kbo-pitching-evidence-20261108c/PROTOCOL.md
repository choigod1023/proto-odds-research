# Frozen retrospective starter-history protocol

Commit before evaluating new candidate outcomes. Reuse only existing official normalized pitching boxes from `kbo-reliever-workload-20261106/official-workload.json.gz`, and existing `pregame-baseball-20261104` exported actual-training M0 and exact-ID price/outcome overlaps. Target-game starter identities come from retrospective box scores: never call them prospectively available or confirmed before first pitch. Only history from D-10 to D-1 is allowed; exclude same-day earlier games and all incomplete prior scheduled games. To address left censoring require all ten calendar days in the cache and each starter to have at least one prior appearance. Same team and numeric player IDs only. Two features, home minus away: pitches in the last ten days divided by 150, days since last appearance divided by 10. This is a new starter history diagnostic, distinct from the earlier top-three reliever workload model; no lineup adjacency or previously tested probability-only calibrations.

Two fixed ridge logistic residual candidates use these identical features: fixed M0 logit offset and fixed normalized market logit offset, with no intercept; penalty 0.1 times squared coefficients. Fit on the complete eligible 2025 dates through April 8 inclusive; validate April 9-15, without changing features or regularization. Refit on both 2025 periods once, then evaluate all eligible 2026 days with same fixture, winner, price and settlement. Model selection is not based on 2026. Show validation even if too small; this is a feasibility diagnostic, not an adoption gate. A 2025 lack of prices or 2026 lack of exact outcomes/prices excludes a game from all arms; report coverage by stage and no-later-price fill. Draw refunds on full game two-way, other uncertain settlements pending/excluded. Probability loss on decisive games only; unit ROI denominator includes refunds. Report selection switches, mean prices, accuracy, ROI, log loss, binary Brier, daily-close MDD, paired day-block bootstrap 2000 seed 20261108 for accuracy, ROI, LL and Brier. Report all time splits and exclusions. Source hashes and player-ID matching; no operational inference or schedule changes.

The former source covers opening 25 days of each season, with prior study having inspected 2026 results; it is not an independent test. Past box response receipts are after events; 2025 selected price cohort excludes draws. Existing M0 forecasts use retrospective actual lineups and seasonal model definitions; the current market-recommendation M0 is not reproduced. Split and choice differ from requested 2025 train/validation => 2026 only as cache availability requires. No claim of practical profitability or validated player effects.
