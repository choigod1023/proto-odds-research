# Frozen M1 audit: retrospective, not a service promotion

## Identity and scope

The +7.64% result is **projected-training M1**, evaluated with `official_batting_and_starter` retrospective roster inputs; it is not the projected evaluation scenario. Its ID in the matched ledger is `kbo-player-effects-v2-2026-M1` (the name is a catalog alias, not the separate `v2/model.json`). It is neither actual-training M0, MLB, nor the team-residual model. The final frozen file selected M2 by the preregistered 2025 log loss, not M1: M0 0.866709, M1 0.892211, M2 0.810751 on 736. M1 is a fixed candidate under a retrospective hypothesis, not the selected family or a validated edge. 2026 was previously inspected; 2024–25 were previously researched. The original 197 includes withdrawn F5 and is not used.

The script computes dense linear predictors and Poisson convolution from frozen coefficients and raw source records, independently of the original training/prediction implementation. Its dense equations are adapted from the original project's `independent_verify.py`; this is a separate calculation, not an independently developed statistical model. It reads saved predictions only to compare, and recomputes sides, prices and Decimal settlement from source inputs. It verifies outcome labels against ledger winners, and 103 price pairs, selections and outcomes against the current matched rows. It does not refit or tune. `replay.json` includes all 1,334 scenario/fixture calculations and monthly breakdowns. Maximum three-way probability difference: 2.78e-15; maximum 103 conditional-home difference: 9.44e-16.

## Results and selection effects

On the identical extended day/team price ledger, official-batting-and-starter M1 has 347 hits / 652 decisive, 15 refunded draws, 667 stakes, -87.76 units, ROI **-13.157%**. Within it, the matched 103 has 67/103, +7.87 units, **+7.641%**; its 564-game complement has 280/549 decisive, 15 refunds, -95.63 units, **-16.956%**. Thus +7.64% is a selected subset, not global performance. Projected-input M1 on the same 667 has 363/652, 15 refunds, -64.40 units, -9.655%. ROI denominator includes refunds. Official-input M1 differs from the corresponding projected-training M0 on 37/103 and 215/667; this is not the separate actual-training M0. The 103 result has a different period and selection mechanism: source/author excerpts, unique official ID, matching archived prices, unambiguous product and non-tied author vote are required. Source and author coverage can select particular games, teams, periods and prices. The 103's March–July composition (3, 28, 1, 30, 41) cannot identify a causal author effect or be extrapolated to the August–October complement. Original publication time is a claim; first observation is after the game or unknown, edits/result leakage are unknown, and archived price receipt time and roster receipt are unproven. Strict pregame cohort: 0. Historical sellability is not established.

Official-input M1 103 monthly ROI: March -54.67% (3), April -9.18% (28), May +97.00% (1), June +22.27% (30), July +10.80% (41). Full 667 monthly ROI: March -42.40%, April -21.16%, May -14.70%, June -8.71%, July -4.43%, August -20.92%, September +2.30%, October -32.48%. These descriptive values have small and uneven cohorts; see the JSON for counts, monthly complement, and projected scenario.

Among the 103, M1 selected **zero** prices <1.20 or ≤1.20. In all 667 official-input rows it selected one <1.20 (1 win at 1.18), and three ≤1.20 (3 wins); neither estimates a reliable low-price edge. Under fixed decimal odds and equal single stakes, with no tax/cost and considering decisive outcomes if draws refund, break-even win rate is 1/odds: 1.20 requires 83.33%. This is algebra, not a profit guarantee. NSW government's [decimal odds teaching material](https://www.gambleaware.nsw.gov.au/-/media/files/maths-stage-4-school-resources/doc23-027419-ga_sr_maths_stage4_lesson02_feb24.ashx?hash=949A2A2FFF241DCFB3B5565D4F83262B&rev=fbf434ca77aa4a74a9d3338ec0904dec) describes implied probability as stake divided by total payout. No official Betman rule for taxes, settlement, or availability was verified here; do not present this simplified calculation as an official product rule.

## Calibration and bounded service contract

The 667 official-input decisive sample has independently computed conditional two-category log loss 0.7178 and two-category-sum Brier 0.5195 (652 decisions); observed home share 0.5184 versus mean home probability 0.5046. The unconditional Poisson draw prediction is structurally high: original 709 projected study reports 12.61% M0 vs 2.26% actual draws, and M1 is also independent Poisson. Mean agreement is not calibration at the tails. This retrospective, selected, non-blind sample, with documented oracle and receipt uncertainties and at most three low-price selections, does **not** establish calibration sufficient for EV-based alternative picks. Normalized inverse odds are market-derived comparisons, not ground-truth calibrated probabilities, and the matched fixed market 90% + votes 10% is multiplicative inverse odds, not necessarily production Shin. Do not use EV to replace a low-odds pick based on this evidence.

A future bounded, fail-closed research replay needs: exact versioned model/recipe and all hashes; complete unique event/market/team identity and original price/market semantics; bounded nine ordered unique hitter IDs and opposing starter ID with role-specific mappings; all 11 raw columns, frozen transforms and provenance including null vs unknown; source snapshots actually received before the applicable T-30 cutoff, same-day leakage prevention, verified official results for settlement, finite numerical inputs and probability mass, explicit tie/refund handling, input/output hash and retrospective flags. Reject missing/ambiguous fixture, market, timing, model, malformed roster, hash, price, or probability; do not silently substitute model, roster, market or alternative. Valid historically missing values can use only the original missing transform once the complete input contract is verified; absent input evidence is not equivalent to a known null. Limit rows and bytes and perform no network, retraining, wallet, scheduler or ledger mutation inside inference. Production currently uses `tipster-vote-v1`; research results do not authorize policy change or rewriting frozen revisions. Actual live feed mapping, training/feature source history and timestamp evidence have not been proven sufficient to reproduce this retrospective oracle in service. The main service worktree had no M1/low-odds runtime files visible when inspected; no runtime implementation was modified here.
